# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 26 October 2026_

Instant Playlist ("the app") is a music player for audio files you already
have on your device. This policy explains what the app does with your data.

## Data we collect

**None.** The app does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal
data or usage data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no
tracking, and it does not connect to any server.

## Data on your device

- **Your music files.** The app reads the audio files in the folders you
  choose so it can play them and show their titles and artists. When you
  tag a song, the tag is written into that song file's own metadata (its
  "genre" field). Your files never leave your device.
- **Chosen folders.** The app remembers which folders you picked so your
  library is still there the next time you open it. This is stored only on
  your device and is removed when you uninstall the app.

## Permissions

- **Music and audio (Android) / chosen folders (iOS):** to find and play
  your songs and to save tags into them.
- **Notifications and background playback:** to keep playing music and show
  playback controls when the app isn't on screen.

## Children

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page
with a new "Last updated" date.
